Penix support

Contact Us

We welcome questions about your account, privacy, community safety, purchases, or the Penix experience.

How can we help?

Send a clear description of your question and include the email address associated with your Penix account when relevant. For safety concerns, you can also use the report and block controls available in the app.

Email support

We aim to review messages as soon as reasonably possible.

penix@gmail.com

Before contacting us

  • For purchase questions, include the product and transaction details shown by Apple, but never send payment credentials.
  • For an account issue, describe the action you were trying to complete and any message shown in the app.
  • For privacy requests, state whether you want access, correction, or deletion assistance.

Community safety

If another member or a piece of content concerns you, use the in-app reporting option so the relevant account and content information can be included accurately. Blocking is available when you no longer want to interact with a member.
